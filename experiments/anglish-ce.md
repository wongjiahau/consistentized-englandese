# Experiment: CE on an Anglish base

Status: experiment, not part of the specification.

This file translates the example texts of SPEC.md again. It keeps every CE affix rule, but it replaces learned roots from Latin, French and Greek with native English roots.

## Rules used

- **Replace** a learned or derived loan when a native word or a part-by-part calque exists: *inspect* → *check*, *employee* → *hiree*, *legislative* → *lawizeive*.
- **Keep** an everyday loan that has no affix inside it: *machine*, *part*, *oven*, *cable*, *trust*, *safe*.
- **Keep** names, titles, SI units and IUPAC names (P6, place names).
- Foreign **affixes** are allowed (`-ment`, `-ion`, `-ize`, `trans-`, `inter-`).
- Words marked † are free coinages, not part-by-part calques. They break the "the rules choose each form" idea.

## F. Universal Declaration of Human Rights (Appendix F)

### Article 1

> All human bements are beared free and even in worthyness and rights. They are bewitted and beinwitted and should interdeal in a mood of brotherness.

- *equal* → *even*; *reason* → *wit*; *conscience* → *inwit* (Middle English, as in *Ayenbite of Inwyt*); *interact* → *interdeal*; *spirit* → *mood*.

### Article 2

> Everyone is betitled to all the rights and freenesses setted forth in this Outsayion†, without sunderment of any kind, such as race, colour, sex, tongue, belief, statecraftal or other thought, folkal or societyal root, ownee, bearment or other standness. Furthermore, no sunderment shall be maked on the ground of the statecraftal, lawsayal or interfolkal standness of the land or holddom† to which a person belongs, whether it be selfstandent, trust, unselfruling or under any other boundment of overlordness.

- *Declareion* → *Outsayion*†; *distinguishment* → *sunderment*; *language* → *tongue*; *religion* → *belief*; *political* → *statecraftal*; *national* → *folkal*; *origin* → *root*; *status* → *standness*; *basis* → *ground*; *jurisdictional* → *lawsayal*; *international* → *interfolkal*; *country* → *land*; *territory* → *holddom*†; *undependent* → *selfstandent*; *unselfgoverning* → *unselfruling*; *limitment* → *boundment*; *sovereignness* → *overlordness*.

### Article 3

> Everyone haves the right to life, freeness and safeness of person.

### Article 4

> No one shall be holded in thrallness or bondness; thrallizement and the thrall-trade shall be forbidded in all their shapes.

- *slave* → *thrall*; *serveorness* → *bondness*; *prohibited* → *forbidded*; *forms* → *shapes*.

### Article 5

> No one shall be subjected to rackment or to grim, unhuman or lowizing handlement or punishment.

- *torture* → *rackment*; *cruel* → *grim*; *degrading* → *lowizing* (`-ize`: make low); *treatment* → *handlement*.
- *subjected* stays. *underput* would mean "put too little", because CE `under-` is only an amount.

## G. Constitution of the United States (Appendix G)

### Preamble

> We the Folkage of the United States, so as to shape a wholer Oneness, set up Rightness, make sure of homeal Stillness, see to the shared wardment, further the overall Welfare, and make fast the Blessions of Freeness to ourselves and our Postkinage, do lay down and set up this Ground-law† for the United States of America.

- *People* → *Folkage* (a base noun names one person, so the group takes `-age`); *Uniteion* → *Oneness*; *Justness* → *Rightness*; *Tranquilness* → *Stillness*; *defendment* → *wardment*; *promote* → *further*; *Descendorage* → *Postkinage*; *Constituteion* → *Ground-law*† (as German *Grundgesetz*).

### Article I, Section 1

> All lawizeive Mights herein gived shall be lieized in a Moot† of the United States, which shall be maked of an Eldermoot† and House of Folk-speakors.

- *Powers* → *Mights*; *granted* → *gived*; *vested* → *lieized* (`-ize` causative of *lie*); *Congress* → *Moot*†; *Senate* → *Eldermoot*† (from Latin *senex*, "old man"); *Representors* → *Folk-speakors*.

## Findings

1. **The affix system works on native roots without change.** No new affix and no new allomorph was needed.
2. **Some changes remove CE problems:**
   - *ruleor* removes the *governor* collision (§5);
   - *bringments* removes the false affix in *deliver*;
   - *Oneness* is easier to read than *Uniteion*.
3. **Some changes lose meaning:**
   - *bribement* is narrower than *corruptment*;
   - *belief* is wider than *religion*;
   - *Moot* does not say that the meeting makes laws.
4. **Free coinages are needed where a word names an institution:** *Outsayion*, *holddom*, *Ground-law*, *Moot*, *Eldermoot*. These are exactly the words where two writers would choose different forms.
5. **Readability drops most in legal text.** Technical text (A, B) stays easy to read. The Declaration and the Constitution become hard to recognize.
6. **SPEC.md has two errors.** Appendix C uses *said* and *met*. The CE forms are *sayed* and *meeted*.
